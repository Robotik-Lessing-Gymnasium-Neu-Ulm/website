# 6 Teams qualified for the German Championships
The regional qualification tournament in Vöhringen is the beginning of the robotics season. With 9 participating 
teams our goal was to present all the skills and improvements we made.

## Soccer
Three teams of ours participated in the soccer category. Team Faabs, the Worldchampions of last year entered again in the open discipline. Where as the Teams Robotronic and Mathimazierer in Soccer Lightweight participated. 
There where no further challenges for the reigning Worldchampions, so they easily qualified for the national championships and also took the first place. As Team Faabs, Team Robotronic, who where also at the World Championships last year. The youngest team, Mathimazierer, took home a third place which is also a qualification ticket. 

## OnStage
Since there where no opponents in the on stage discipline, the only goal was to perform in the best possible way. After some troubles in the beginning the team startet to improve very quickly. The performances where very entertaining, caught the attention of the spectator an showed that work pays off. There are more improvements planned for the german championships, so that the team can present themselves in full strength.

## Rescue
For the first time ever we also participated in the rescue maze disciplin. The goal for that category is to navigate trough a maze and to drop rescue kits at pre specified points. Here showed the team NEBula, built around team member Bahaa Alia, that they are willing to learn and improve in this (for us) new disciplin. NEBula made third place. Their  Das Team landete auf dem 3.Platz. Their succesors participated in the entry category, what means, that the participation age isa limited. Sadly the boys took sixth place, what means that they missed the qualification by one place.

Also in the category rescue, but by following a line(Rescue Line Entry), our youngest groups tried their best. The 5th and 6th class students conquered different parcours and lines with their Lego robots. Out of the three registered teams, one team made the qualification. To all the others: Heads up, many more years of robotic will follow ;)
